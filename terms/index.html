Mitla shows what takes up space on your disk and helps you remove files you choose: caches, logs, temporary files, developer data, apps and their leftovers, and any other files or folders you select. Mitla suggests what is usually safe to remove, but the decision is always yours. Nothing is removed without your confirmation.

2. Your responsibility

Before you confirm a removal, check the list of items. In particular:

  • Make sure you have a current backup of your Mac (for example, with Time Machine) before cleaning up.

  • Removing caches or app data can sign you out of apps, reset their settings or make them rebuild data, which may take time.
  • Removing an app also removes the related files Mitla found; review that list before confirming.
