Questions

What does Mitla mean?

It's Ukrainian for "broom". Say it "MEET-la".

Why does Mitla ask for Full Disk Access?

macOS hides some folders, like Mail and other apps' data, from every app. With Full Disk Access Mitla can show their size too. Without it Mitla still works and simply skips them.

Why isn't it in the Mac App Store?

App Store apps run in a sandbox that can't see your whole disk. Mitla is signed with an Apple Developer ID and notarized by Apple, so macOS opens it without warnings.

Does Mitla collect any data?

No. It has no analytics or tracking, and goes online only to check for updates. See the Privacy Policy.
